About Staafin

A focused HRMS for teams that run on people

Staafin is being built for organisations that need clearer control over employee records, leave, attendance, shifts, assets and workforce reporting.

Our purpose

Many growing teams still manage HR operations through spreadsheets, messages and disconnected tools. That creates avoidable admin work and makes it harder for managers to see what is happening day to day.

Staafin brings the operational side of HR into one system, with a practical interface for care providers, staffing teams and multi-site SMEs.

How we want Staafin to feel

Clear enough for busy managers to use during real operational work.

Structured enough for owners and HR teams to maintain proper oversight.

Honest enough to show the product clearly without inflated claims.

Product principles

The standards guiding the platform as it grows.

1

Operational clarity

Every module should help managers understand the state of the workforce quickly.

2

Controlled records

Employee data, documents and permissions should stay organised and accountable.

3

Practical adoption

The product should support real teams without forcing unnecessary complexity into daily work.
