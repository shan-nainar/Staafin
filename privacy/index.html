Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal information submitted through this website is handled. Staafin is a product and trading name of Elite Care and Support Ltd, which is the data controller for this website.

Who we are

Elite Care and Support Ltd (Company No. 14303034), registered in England & Wales. Registered office: The Work Place, Heighington Lane, Aycliffe Business Park, Newton Aycliffe, County Durham, DL5 6AH. Privacy enquiries: connect@staafin.co.uk — please include "Privacy request" in your message.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit through forms or email links (name, work email, company name, enquiry type and message).
  • Company and role information you provide when requesting a demo or pricing.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends to any website (such as IP address and browser type) in server logs retained by our hosting provider.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, arrange product discussions and understand what your organisation needs. Our lawful bases are legitimate interest (responding to enquiries you initiate) and, if you become a customer, performance of a contract. We do not sell enquiry data and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Retention

Enquiry emails are retained for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless a longer period is required for legal or contractual reasons.

Cookies

This website does not currently set non-essential cookies or use third-party analytics. If analytics or similar tools are added in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required before setting them.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where required to operate this website (e.g. our hosting provider acting as a data processor) or where required by law.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. To exercise any right, email connect@staafin.co.uk with "Privacy request" in the subject line.
