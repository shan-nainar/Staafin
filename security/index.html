Security & Compliance

Staafin holds sensitive workforce data, so security is part of the product — not an afterthought. Here's how we protect it.

Your data controller

Staafin is a product of Elite Care and Support Ltd (Company No. 14303034, England & Wales), which is the data controller for customer data. Our ICO registration reference is available on request, and each customer receives a data processing agreement before onboarding.

Role-based access control

Access in Staafin is role-based: owners, HR teams, managers and employees each see only what their responsibilities require. Permissions and approval workflows are configured per organisation, and sensitive records carry an audit trail of who viewed or changed them.

Encrypted connections

All traffic between your browser and Staafin is encrypted in transit using TLS, and this website enforces HTTPS. Product-level encryption at rest, backup schedules and retention periods are documented in each customer's data processing agreement.

Working-time evidence

Attendance, timesheets and manager approvals are kept together as structured records, so working-time decisions are based on evidence. Right-to-work and key employee documents are organised with review dates, supporting UK employment compliance.

UK GDPR principles, honestly stated

Staafin is designed around UK GDPR principles: data minimisation (only the fields your workflows need), purpose limitation, storage limitation, and accountability through structured records and access controls. We don't claim certifications we don't hold — instead, we publish what we do and make our documentation available for your review during procurement. If you need a DPIA, sub-processor list or security questionnaire completed, we can provide those during onboarding.

Questions about security or data protection?

Email us and we'll put you in touch with the team — including DPA requests, sub-processor questions and security reviews.
